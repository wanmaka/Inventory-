1. Sequence `048–052` was not observed in the current PDF Swimlane folder during this import; AI must treat those numbers as `UNKNOWN`, not missing by assumption.

3. Earthquake has at least two swimlanes (`042`, `043`) with different operational scope; do not merge them without checking the source content.
4. Protester/Rioting are represented separately (`044_Rioting`, `053_Protester Management`) and should remain separate operational scenarios unless the SOP explicitly links them.
5. The library contains both Security/Emergency and Building/System failure flows; cross-reference must be domain-aware rather than assuming every item is owned by SOC.
